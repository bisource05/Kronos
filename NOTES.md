## What it is

A foundation model trained on candlestick data that simulates many possible next paths for a chart, so you can see how often the paths end higher and how wide the range of outcomes is.

## Caution

Use it as a filter on ideas you already like, not as a signal on its own. Raw forecasts still need market and style effects stripped out, and fees and slippage modelled, before they count as alpha.

## Status

Forked Oct 27, 2026. Nothing here has been run, configured, or connected to any account, API key, or real money. Paper / dry-run first, and only after a walk-forward backtest with fees, a look-ahead check, and 30 days on paper.
